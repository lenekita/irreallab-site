About irreallab

irreallab is a collection of surreal visual experiments created by Radu Nechita using artificial intelligence, generative tools, and digital technology. Each reel is a unique exploration of motion, color, and visual abstraction, designed to challenge perception and inspire imagination.

The music featured in these visual experiments is the intellectual property of the respective artists and rights holders. This work is used to enhance and complement our creative visual storytelling. All music credits are provided to honor the original artists and creators. For licensing inquiries or copyright concerns, please contact us at the contact page.
