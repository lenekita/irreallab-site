How to get Instagram video URL:

  1. Go to your reel on Instagram (open in browser, not app)
  2. Right-click the video → Inspect (or press F12)
  3. Find the <video> tag in the HTML
  4. Look for src="https://..." - copy that URL (the whole long link)
  5. Paste it below
  6. Click "Extract & Format"
  7. Copy the formatted JSON
The URL should start with https://scontent...
